So you don't want a Trump Republican as your senator. Before you vote, here's what a Bodnar seat can and can't do.

Seth Bodnar has pledged not to caucus with either party. In the Senate, that means he doesn't count toward a Democratic majority, gets no say in who's majority leader, and has to rely on party leaders he spent his campaign attacking to give him committee seats. Committees are where bills are written, money is attached, and subpoenas are issued.

Criticizing Trump isn't the same as checking him. A real check takes a majority, a committee seat, and a gavel.
