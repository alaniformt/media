The last time a senator tried this

1953: Wayne Morse refused to join either party.

He was stripped of his Armed Services and Labor seats and left with the low-profile spots party leaders chose to give him. When he forced a vote to get them back, 7 senators backed him.

He got his choice of committees back in 1955, only after he joined the Democrats.

The bottom line

Criticism isn't a check on power.

A check takes a majority, a committee seat, and a gavel. Bodnar has pledged away the one thing that gets all three.

Ask him: if your vote decides the Senate majority, which side gets it?
